Research areas: Investments · Institutional Investors · AI & FinTech

I am a fifth-year PhD candidate in Finance at the University of Florida. My research studies investment management, institutional investors, capital markets, and the use of artificial intelligence in finance.

I earned an MS in Finance and a BS in Mathematics and Statistics, with a minor in Computer Science, from the University of Florida. Before beginning the PhD program, I worked in investment research at River Road Asset Management.

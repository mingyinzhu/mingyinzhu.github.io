Research

Working papers

My research examines investment products and investor behavior, as well as how artificial intelligence changes financial analysis and economic measurement.

Reaching for Synthetic Yield

with Wei Jiang, Yuehua Tang, and Yanbin Wu

We study the growth of covered-call funds and show how investment companies manufacture distributions through option writing. Investors reward high headline yields with inflows without fully distinguishing asset-generated income from option-based distributions, while the strategies cap upside and affect option prices.

The Memorization Problem: Can We Trust LLMs' Economic Forecasts?

with Alejandro Lopez-Lira and Yuehua Tang

We evaluate whether large language models recall economic and financial information from their training periods. Selective recall can make historical forecasting and backtesting appear more successful than genuine out-of-sample prediction, creating a fundamental measurement problem for empirical work using LLMs.
